HOW TO PLAY

A serpent of colored runes marches toward the maw.
Fire runes from the eye at its center. Match three
or more of the same color to break the chain.

Controls:

Aim        Mouse move / Arrow keys
Fire       Left click / Space
Swap       Right click / Shift
Pause      Esc

Powerups:
Backtrack · Blaster · Colorshift · Slowmo
