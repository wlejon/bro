How to Play

You have a marble spout and a goal cup. Your job: place pieces so a marble released from the spout ends up in the cup.

Camera: right-drag orbit, middle-drag pan, wheel zoom.

Build: select a piece (1–7), click a cell to place it, right-click to remove. R rotates, Q/E change layer height.

Run: press Space. Marbles drop from the spout; the timer starts on release and stops when the first marble lands in the cup.

Pieces: Block is solid ground. Ramp slopes. Wall blocks lanes. Bumper bounces. Spinner rotates and flings. Booster pushes marbles along its facing direction. Chute funnels marbles toward its center.

Each level has a piece budget and a par time. Beat par for gold.
