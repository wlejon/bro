HOW TO PLAY

TEND: click water to drop pellets. Fish eat pellets and drop coins. Click coins to collect.

DEFEND: click intruders to damage them. Larger ones take more hits.

SHOP: between days, buy more fish, upgrades, and pet eggs.

SURVIVE: a day lasts 2 minutes. Keep your fish alive across 20+ days.

CONTROLS: Click to feed/collect/attack. Space to drop pellets. 1-5 to buy. P to pause.
