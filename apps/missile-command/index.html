HOW TO PLAY

Enemy ICBMs are falling on your six cities.

Aim with the mouse, click to launch a counter-missile from the nearest silo.

Its warhead detonates at your cursor — explosions destroy any enemy caught inside, and can chain.

Each silo has 10 missiles per wave. Lose a silo if it's struck.

Game over when all cities are gone. Bonus at wave end for surviving cities and unused ammo.
