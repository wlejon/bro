HOW TO PLAY

Grab & drag any puff.
Drag horizontally to slide the whole row — cells that exit one side reappear on the other.
Drag vertically to slide the whole column the same way.

Release to snap. Any line of 3+ same-color puffs in a row or column POPS. Gravity drops the remaining puffs. New puffs float in from the top.

Scoring: 50 per puff · cascades multiply x1, x2, x3…

Specials:
Match 4 in a line → Jumbo Puff (clears 3×3)
Match 5 in a line → Arrow Puff (clears its row/column)
L or T shape → Prism Puff (clears all of that color)

Locked puffs have a clamp — they freeze the row or column they sit in. Match them to set them free.

Controls:
Mouse: click-drag a puff. Direction locks after a few pixels.
Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Space to grab, arrows to slide, Space to release.
Esc to pause.
