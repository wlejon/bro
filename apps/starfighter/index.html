BRIEFING

The Dominion has completed its planetoid fortress, the Citadel. One unshielded exhaust vent reaches its reactor core. Ignite the reactor and the Citadel falls.

THREE SECTORS — you will cycle through them as long as you survive:

1.  SPACE. Enemy fighters. Dogfight. Watch for the Black Ace.
2.  SURFACE. Towers, bunkers, catwalks. Strafe and destroy.
3.  TRENCH. Barriers, turrets. Hit the vent dead center.
