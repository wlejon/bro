HOW TO PLAY

HOW TO PLAY

Watch the sequence of pads light up.

Repeat the sequence by clicking the pads
in the same order.

Each round adds one more step.

One wrong pad ends the game.

Controls

1 or Q  ·  Red (top-left)
2 or W  ·  Green (top-right)
3 or A  ·  Yellow (bottom-left)
4 or S  ·  Blue (bottom-right)
