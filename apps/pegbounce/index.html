HOW TO PLAY

Aim with the mouse. Click or press Space to launch the ball.

Gravity pulls the ball down through a field of pegs. Every peg it hits lights up and bursts at the end of the shot.

Peg colors:
Blue — bonus score
Orange — clear all to win the level
Green — triggers your guide's ability
Purple — doubles score for the rest of the shot

A catch bar scrolls along the bottom. Land in it to win a free ball.

Controls: Mouse aims, click launches. Or Left/Right + Space. Esc to pause.
