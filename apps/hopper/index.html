HOW TO PLAY

Use arrow keys to hop one tile at a time.

Dodge cars and trucks on the road.

Ride logs across the river - stepping in water drowns you.

Fill all 5 lily pads at the top to win the round.

Scoring: +10 for each forward hop, +50 per pad,
bonus for leftover time.
