HOW TO PLAY

Slide all tiles in a direction using
arrow keys or WASD.

When two tiles of the same number touch,
they merge into one tile with double the value.

You score points equal to the merged value.

Reach the 2048 tile to win!
Keep playing for a higher score if you like.

Press U to undo one move.
Press R to restart. Esc to menu.
